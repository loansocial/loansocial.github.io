Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

What we collect

  • What you type into our free-posts form: first name and email.
  • If you subscribe: your name, email and payment status from our checkout provider, Whop. We never see or store your card number.
  • Brand details you give us after checkout: logo, headshot, brand colors, name, title, NMLS ID, company and company NMLS ID, work email, phone, Instagram handle, city and state, and notes on how you write.
  • If you add the Realtor co-branded pack: your partner agent's name, title, headshot, logo, brokerage and licence number. You confirm your partner has agreed to this.
  • Basic site activity through the Meta Pixel and cookies: pages viewed, form submitted, checkout started and completed.

How we use it

  • To make and send your free posts and, if you subscribe, your monthly posts and add-ons.
  • To email you about your posts, your subscription and our offer. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To measure and improve our ads on Facebook and Instagram.

We do not sell your information.

Who processes it for us

  • Brevo: email and contact list.
  • Cloudflare: receives form submissions and stores your brand files and finished posts.
  • Whop: checkout and subscription billing.
  • Meta Platforms: ad measurement through the Meta Pixel.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.

Cookies and the Meta Pixel

This site uses the Meta Pixel, which may set cookies and share events such as page views, form submissions and purchases with Meta. You can control ad personalization in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings, or block third-party cookies in your browser.

Borrower information

Please do not send us borrower or client information: names, loan files, credit details or contact lists. We never need it. The past-client newsletter is sent by you, from your own email or CRM, so your client list never leaves your hands.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your details while you are a lead or subscriber and for up to 24 months after. Email hello@getloansocial.com to see, correct or delete what we hold, and we will reply within 30 days.

Content compliance: your review and approval

Mortgage advertising is regulated, and the rules differ by state, by lender and by license. You, the loan officer, and your company are responsible for the final review and approval of every post, caption and email before you publish it, including under state and federal advertising rules: NMLS identification and state licensing disclosures, Equal Housing Opportunity requirements, RESPA (including co-marketing with real estate agents), and the Truth in Lending Act and Regulation Z (including advertising trigger terms, which require additional disclosures).

We put an NMLS and Equal Housing Opportunity footer on every post and write copy that avoids rate promises and trigger terms, but we are not your compliance department and nothing we deliver is legal advice. Have your compliance team review each month before you post. Ask for any change and we will make it.

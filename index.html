Questions

Before you start

Do I need Canva Pro?

No. The templates open in free Canva. Pro makes branding faster because you can apply your brand kit to every post at once.

Is the content compliant?

Every post carries an NMLS and Equal Housing Opportunity footer, and the copy avoids rate promises and advertising trigger terms. Rules differ by lender and state, so your compliance team should still review before you post. LoanSocial does not replace them.

How are the captions in my voice?

When you join, you answer a few questions about how you talk to clients. Captions are written to match, and the AI prompts let you rewrite any of them in seconds.

When does each month arrive?

By the 25th of the month before, so you have time to review it with compliance before the 1st. Create members get December's posts now; January's land by 25 December. On Branded AI your 3 previews arrive within one business day of setup and your first month within 2 business days of approving them; after that, every month by the 25th.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from your Whop account. If you cancel during the 7-day trial you are not charged.

What are the 3 free previews?

After you join Branded AI and add your logo, colors, headshot and NMLS details, we make 3 real posts in your brand within one business day. They're yours to keep and post. They don't count as using your plan.

What's the refund policy on Branded AI?

Full refund within 7 days of purchase as long as you haven't started your first monthly batch. Previews don't count. Once you approve your previews and start your month, you can still cancel anytime and you won't be billed again.

How does the Realtor co-branded pack work?

Each post also carries your partner agent's name, headshot, brokerage and licence number. Agree with your partner how you'll share the cost, and have your compliance team check co-marketing (RESPA) rules for your company before you post.

Do you send the newsletter to my clients?

No. Each month you get a ready-to-send email in your brand, and you send it from your own email or CRM. Your client list never leaves your hands.

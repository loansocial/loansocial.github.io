Refund policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

We want this to be simple. Here is exactly when you get money back.

Branded AI: full refund within 7 days if unused

You get a full refund if you ask within 7 days of purchase and you haven't started your first monthly batch. Your first monthly batch starts when you approve your 3 preview posts and press "make my month" on your member page. The 3 free preview posts never count as use, and you can keep them either way.

This applies to the monthly and yearly Branded AI plans and to introductory offers such as $37 for the first month. If you get this refund, we also refund any add-on you bought with Branded AI that hasn't been delivered yet.

Create: 7-day free trial

The Create monthly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. Cancel during the trial and you are never charged. Once a Create payment has gone through, that period is not refunded, because the month's templates are delivered straight away.

After you've started

Once your first Branded AI month has started, or a Create period has been paid, that payment is not refunded. You can still cancel anytime and you won't be billed again; you keep everything already delivered.

Add-ons and the Profile kit

The Realtor co-branded pack and the past-client newsletter follow the plan they're added to. The Profile kit ($27 one time) is refunded in full if we haven't delivered it; once delivered it is not refunded.

If we miss a delivery

If we fail to deliver a month you have paid for, we refund that month in full.

How to ask

Email hello@getloansocial.com from the email you checked out with, and say which plan. We reply within one business day. Refunds go back to your original payment method through Whop and usually show within 5 to 10 business days.

Cancel instead?

To stop future payments without a refund request, cancel here in a minute. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
